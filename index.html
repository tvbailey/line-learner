How it works

Made-up words drill runs only your lines with the film's made-up curse words: the cue, then you, then the words flash big so you can check yourself. In a regular run, each of those lines shows the script's spelling beside what the phone heard, and the end of the scene lists them all. The phone can only tell whether they sound close; you're the judge of exact.

With Dialogue only on, the phone skips everything between a song's start and end, spoken lines inside the song included, and picks up at the next dialogue. With it off: you practice the first two sung lines after each lead-in; the rest of the song is skipped, and the last sung line before your next spoken line is read as your cue. After a scene, open "Scene around this line": ✓ Matched lines were right, ? Check lines were probably the phone mishearing you, × Needs work lines need work.

Voice test checklist
  1. Tap Start and allow the microphone. The phone reads Mother's line; say yours when it goes quiet.
  2. On one of your lines, say "line" instead. It gives the first few words. Say "line" again for the whole line.
  3. On the line marked (pause), stop for about three seconds in the middle. Does it wait, or jump ahead? If it jumps, raise the "wait before moving on" setting.
  4. Say "pause" on its own; then say "resume". Say "repeat" to hear the last cue again.
  5. Two of your lines contain the word "pause" in a sentence. It should not pause there.
  6. Run it once more in the car, phone in the dash mount.
  7. Tap Copy report and paste it to Reginald, with a sentence on how it felt.
